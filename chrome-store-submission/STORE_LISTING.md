### Full Description

**Protect Your Job Search from Scams - Powered by AI**

ApplySafe is an intelligent job scam detector that analyzes job postings in real-time as you browse popular job boards. Using advanced AI analysis and H1B verification data, ApplySafe identifies suspicious job listings before you waste time applying.

**Key Features:**

🤖 **AI-Powered Scam Detection**
Instantly analyze job postings using advanced machine learning to identify red flags and scam indicators. Get risk scores (0-100) for each job to understand how likely it is to be fraudulent.

🌐 **Works Across All Major Job Sites**
LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Monster, Dice, and more. Click "Check this job" on any listing to get instant analysis.

✅ **H1B Sponsorship Verification**
Verify if companies actually sponsor H1B visas by cross-referencing with real H1B visa application data. Know which companies truly support visa sponsorship before applying.

🔐 **Privacy First**
All analysis happens securely. Your data is never sold. Optional cloud sync keeps your scan history across devices.

📊 **Dashboard Analytics**
Track your job search:
- Daily scan activity monitoring
- Scam detection statistics
- Top job sources by safety rating
- Complete scan history with search & filter

🎯 **AI Tools (Pro)**
Unlock advanced AI features:
- **Cover Letter Generator** - Create personalized cover letters based on job descriptions
- **Resume Analyzer** - Get AI feedback on how well your resume matches the job
- **Interview Prep** - Practice answers to common interview questions
- **AI Chat** - Ask questions about job applications and careers

⭐ **Company Whitelist**
Mark trusted companies to never get alerts from them again. Build your personal list of verified safe employers.

🔔 **Smart Notifications**
Get alerts when high-risk jobs are detected. Customize notification preferences.

💾 **Secure Data Sync**
Sync your data across devices with end-to-end encrypted cloud backup (Pro feature).

## Permissions Explained

- **activeTab, tabs** - To analyze the current job posting you're viewing
- **storage** - To save your scan history and preferences locally
- **notifications** - To alert you when scams are detected
- **identity** - To securely sign in with your Google account (optional)
- **Host permissions** - To access job postings on LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, etc.

## Getting Started

1. Install ApplySafe from the Chrome Web Store
2. Visit any job board (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, etc.)
3. Find a job posting you want to check
4. Click the ApplySafe extension icon → "Check This Job"
5. Get instant analysis and risk score
6. Review your scan history in the dashboard
