# Privacy Policy for ApplySafe

**Last Updated:** December 14, 2025

## Introduction

ApplySafe ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Job Posting Data**: When you analyze a job, we temporarily process the job title, description, company name, and URL to provide scam detection analysis.
- **Usage Statistics**: Anonymous data about feature usage (number of scans, upgrade clicks) to improve our service.
- **Subscription Status**: Your trial status, scan count, and subscription state stored locally in your browser.

### Information You Provide
- **Payment Information**: When you upgrade to Pro, Stripe processes your payment details. We do not store credit card information.
- **Contact Information**: Email address associated with your subscription (processed by Stripe).

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Analyze job postings for scam detection using AI
- Verify H1B sponsorship data from public sources
- Manage your subscription and trial status
- Provide customer support
- Improve our extension features

## Data Storage

- **Local Storage**: Subscription status, scan history, and job analysis results are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.
- **Cloud Processing**: Job data is sent to our secure servers only during analysis and is not permanently stored.
- **H1B Data Cache**: Company H1B verification results are cached locally for 7 days to reduce API calls.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Anthropic Claude AI**: For job scam analysis (privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
- **Stripe**: For payment processing (privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy)
- **h1bdata.info**: For H1B verification data (publicly available information)

## Data Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with advertisers
- Track your browsing activity outside job analysis
- Store analyzed job data permanently

We MAY share information:
- When required by law
- To protect our rights and safety
- With service providers (Anthropic, Stripe) to provide core functionality

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your stored data
- Delete your scan history (via extension settings)
- Cancel your subscription at any time
- Request data deletion by contacting us

## Data Retention

- **Scan History**: Stored locally until you clear it
- **Subscription Data**: Retained while subscription is active, deleted 90 days after cancellation
- **Analysis Requests**: Not permanently stored on our servers

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
- Secure API authentication
- No storage of sensitive payment information
- Regular security audits

## Children's Privacy

ApplySafe is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
